## 🌟 Key Features

### 🎯 SMARTER Goal Setting & Tracking
- Define short-term and long-term milestones with precision.
- Track progress daily with integrated health metrics (Steps, Heart Rate via Health Connect) and habit tracking.

### ⏱️ Eisenhower Matrix Productivity
- Prioritize your tasks instantly using the proven Eisenhower Matrix framework (Urgent vs. Important).
- Eliminate distractions and focus on what truly moves the needle.

### 🤖 AI Strategy Consultant & Chat Assistant
- Get instant, actionable advice from your dedicated AI strategy coach.
- Brainstorm ideas, overcome mental blocks, and receive personalized consulting on demand.

### ✨ Daily Inspiration & Affirmations
- Start your day right with curated daily affirmations and tailored "For You" (FYP) recommendations designed to boost your mindset.

### ⚖️ Advanced Decision-Making Aid
- Make complex life and career decisions with confidence using structured pros/cons and multi-criteria evaluation tools.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Architecture

- **AI-Powered Intelligence**: Advanced AI coaching and smart recommendations tailored to your daily growth and productivity goals.
- **Holistic Health & Fitness Sync**: Seamless integration with Android Health Connect to track your steps, heart rate, and wellness milestones right alongside your productivity metrics.
- **Smart Time Management**: Built-in Eisenhower Matrix framework to automatically categorize and prioritize your tasks for maximum daily efficiency.
- **Secure & Private**: Your personal goals, reflections, and health data stay secure and private on your device.
