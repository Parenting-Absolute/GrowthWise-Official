# ⌚ How to Sync Your Smart Watch with GrowthWise

Connect your smart watch to **GrowthWise** to automatically track your daily steps and heart rate. 

GrowthWise uses Google's **Health Connect** to safely bridge data between your watch and the app. Follow these simple steps to get everything set up!

---

## 📋 Before You Begin
Make sure you have the following ready on your Android phone:
* The **GrowthWise** app installed.
* The official **Smart Watch** companion app installed and paired with your watch.
* **Health Connect** (Built-in on Android 14 and above; available on the Google Play Store for older Android versions).

---

## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Setup

### Step 1: Check Health Connect on Your Phone
1. Open your phone's **Settings** and search for **Health Connect** (or open the Health Connect app directly if you downloaded it from the Play Store).
2. Make sure it is set up and ready to use.

### Step 2: Connect Your Smart Watch Companion App to Health Connect
Before GrowthWise can see your fitness data, your Smart Watch Companion app needs permission to send data to Health Connect.
1. Open the **Smart Watch** companion app on your phone.
2. Go to your app's **Settings** or **Profile** menu.
3. Look for **Health Connect**, **Sync**, or **Data Sharing** options.
4. Enable data sharing and ensure **Steps** and **Heart Rate** are turned on.

> **💡 Tip:** You can double-check this in your phone's main settings:  
> Go to **Settings > Apps > Health Connect > App permissions**, tap on your **smart watch's companion app's name** , and make sure **Write** permissions are enabled for *Steps* and *Heart Rate*.

### Step 3: Enable Sync in GrowthWise
Now, let GrowthWise read your fitness data:
1. Open the **GrowthWise** app.
2. Go to the **Goal Tracking** screen.
3. Turn ON the **Health Connect** toggle switch.
4. A permission screen will pop up—select **Steps** and **Heart Rate** (or tap **Allow all**).
5. Tap **Allow** to confirm.

---

## ✅ How to Test Your Connection
Want to make sure everything is working smoothly? Give it a quick test:
1. **Move around:** Wear your smart watch and walk around for a couple of minutes.
2. **Sync your watch:** Open the companion app and pull down on the home screen (or tap sync) to update your latest watch data.
3. **Check GrowthWise:** Open **GrowthWise** and go to your **Goal Tracking** screen. Your new steps and heart rate should appear automatically!

> [!NOTE]
> Data synchronization between your companion app and Health Connect is not always instantaneous. It can occasionally take a few minutes to a few hours for new step counts or heart rate readings to process and populate across your apps.

---

## ❓ Troubleshooting
* **Data not showing up in GrowthWise?** 
  Open the **Health Connect** app on your phone and tap **Data and access**. Check if your recent steps from the companion app are listed there. If they aren't, open the companion app and trigger a manual sync.
* **Accidentally denied permissions?** 
  Go to your phone's **Settings > Apps > Health Connect > App permissions**, select **GrowthWise**, and toggle on **Steps** and **Heart Rate**.
